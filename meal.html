Travel & Stay

Getting there

The nearest station is Moreton-in-Marsh, with direct trains from London Paddington (~1h 40m). From there it's a 15-minute taxi to the venue.

Where to stay

The Lygon Arms — 4 mi, walkable countryside hotel.

Cotswold Cottages — group of self-catered cottages, great if a few of you are coming together.

Parking

Free on-site parking at the barn. Cars can be left overnight and collected by 11 am the next morning — please don't drive home if you've had a drink.

FAQ

What time does the evening end?
Carriages at midnight. The bar closes at 11:30 pm.
Can I bring a plus one?
Plus ones are named on your invitation. If we haven't named yours, we hope you'll forgive us — space is tight.
Are children welcome?
We've kept it to adults for the evening. Babysitting can be arranged at the Lygon Arms if that helps.
Is there a gift registry?
Your presence is the present. If you'd still like to give something, a contribution to our honeymoon fund would be very gratefully received — details on the inside of your invitation.
Will there be food?
Yes — a hot buffet around 10 pm, plus canapés earlier and a midnight snack for the brave.
