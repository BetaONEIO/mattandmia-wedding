Upload Privacy

This page explains how the wedding photo and video uploader handles your information.

What you share

Uploading sends your selected original files, filenames and optional name to the wedding website so Matt and Mia can collect memories of their day. Please only share photos and videos you are comfortable sharing with them and people who can access their collection.

Where uploads go

Cloudflare hosts the website and processes upload requests. New uploads are saved in Matt and Mia’s wedding folder on Google Drive. Files uploaded before the Google Drive connection remain in Cloudflare storage unless moved separately.

The Google Drive folder is shared with anyone who has its link. Files saved there inherit the folder’s sharing settings, so people with that link may be able to view or download them. The upload page itself does not display a gallery.

Google account connection

Guests do not need to sign in to Google. The organiser connects their account once and selects the wedding folder. The app requests access to files and folders selected for it, and files it creates, using Google’s drive.file permission. It uses that access to save wedding uploads. Account credentials are kept on the server and are not sent to guests.

Questions and removal

There is no automatic deletion schedule. To ask about an upload or request its removal, contact admin@betaone.io and include enough detail to identify the file. Copies already downloaded by others cannot be removed by this website.
