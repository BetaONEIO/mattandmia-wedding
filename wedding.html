FAQ

What's a ceilidh?
A live band and a caller who walks everyone through the steps — no experience needed, just comfortable shoes and a willingness to be flung around a barn.
Can I bring a plus one?
Plus ones are named on your invitation. If we haven't named yours, we hope you'll forgive us — numbers are tight.
Are children welcome?
Children named on your invitation are very welcome. If you'd like to bring others, please drop us a message before RSVPing.
What's the dress code?
Smart, but pick something you can dance in — the ceilidh isn't kind to anything too tight or too tall.
