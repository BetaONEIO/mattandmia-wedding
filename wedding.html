Our Story

We met one rainy Tuesday in a coffee shop neither of us was supposed to be in — Matt was hiding from a meeting, Mia was waiting out the weather. Six years, three flats and one slightly unhinged rescue dog later, here we are.

We can't wait to share the day with the people who have shaped our story so far. Thank you for being part of it.

Travel & Stay

Getting there

The nearest station is Moreton-in-Marsh, with direct trains from London Paddington (~1h 40m). From there it's a 15-minute taxi to the venue.

Where to stay

The Lygon Arms — 4 mi, walkable countryside hotel.

Manor House Inn — on-site rooms, limited; ask us before booking.

Cotswold Cottages — group of self-catered cottages, great for families.

Parking

Free on-site parking is available at both the chapel and the barn. Cars can be left overnight and collected by 11 am the next morning.

FAQ

Are children welcome?
We adore your kids, but we've kept the day to adults and immediate family only. Babysitting can be arranged at the Lygon Arms if helpful.
Can I bring a plus one?
Plus ones are named on your invitation. If we haven't named yours, we hope you'll forgive us — space is tight.
Is there a gift registry?
Your presence is the present. If you'd still like to give something, a contribution to our honeymoon fund would be very gratefully received — details on the inside of your invitation.
What's the weather like?
September in the Cotswolds is usually mild but unpredictable. Bring a wrap or jacket for the evening just in case.
Will the ceremony be outdoors?
The ceremony is in the chapel; drinks afterwards are on the lawn (weather permitting), with the barn as a cosy backup.
