Our Story

We met at Harper Adams University, where we were both studying Agriculture and both ended up at Christian Union. We were good friends for a long time before it was anything more — it wasn't until the back end of Mia's placement year that we finally started seeing each other properly.

Matt proposed at the start of April, and we still can't quite believe we get to do this with the people we love most around us. Thank you for being part of it.

FAQ

What's a ceilidh?
A live band and a caller who walks everyone through the steps — no experience needed, just comfortable shoes and a willingness to be flung around a barn.
Can I bring a plus one?
Plus ones are named on your invitation. If we haven't named yours, we hope you'll forgive us — numbers are tight.
Are children welcome?
Children named on your invitation are very welcome. If you'd like to bring others, please drop us a message before RSVPing.
What's the dress code?
Smart, but pick something you can dance in — the ceilidh isn't kind to anything too tight or too tall.
